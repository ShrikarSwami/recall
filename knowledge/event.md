# Q: What is this event?
- also: what's going on here / what is Worn / what is this hackathon / why is everyone here
A: This is Worn, a smart-glasses hackathon. Teams build apps for MicroLumin's M6 glasses over two days.

# Q: What is the theme?
- also: what's the challenge / what's the hackathon theme
A: The theme is "Second nature": wearable AI that understands you and feels effortless.

# Q: What are the tracks?
- also: what tracks are there / which categories can teams pick
A: There are three tracks: Personal Insight, Seamless Handoff, and Natural Interaction.

# Q: Which track is our team in?
- also: what track are we / what track did BB pick
A: Your team, BB, is in Natural Interaction: so natural you forget it's there.

# Q: What room are we in?
- also: which room is this / where is the hackathon / what rooms is it in
A: The hackathon is in rooms A105 and A103 at City Tech, 285 Jay Street.

# Q: Who organized this?
- also: who's running the event / who are the organizers / who put this on
A: Worn is run by localhost:nyc, led by Stephen Jin, with Somya Gupta, Vishal Kharker and Olivia Iarmak.

# Q: Who made these glasses?
- also: who makes the M6 / what company makes the glasses
A: MicroLumin, a team from Shenzhen, makes these M6 glasses. They won awards at CES 2026.

# Q: Who are the sponsors?
- also: who is sponsoring this / who brought this event
A: It's brought to you by MicroLumin, Waftech, localhost:nyc and Wardah, with Cornell Tech student groups.

# Q: Who are the judges?
- also: who is judging / who's on the judging panel
A: Ten judges, including Dr. Dai Zhaoen of MicroLumin, Sam Shear of Yes Hearing, and D.J. Patel.

# Q: How is it judged?
- also: how do the judges score / what are the judging criteria
A: Five things, two points each: understands you, phone-to-glasses flow, ease of use, works on the glasses, and the pitch.

# Q: What are the prizes?
- also: what can we win / what's the prize money
A: First place wins a thousand dollars and three pairs of M6 glasses. Second and third win too.

# Q: When are submissions due?
- also: what's the deadline / when do we have to submit
A: Submissions close at 2 o'clock today. No late submissions.

# Q: Where do we submit?
- also: how do we turn in the project
A: The team submits through the event's Airtable form, before 2 o'clock.

# Q: How does judging work?
- also: what happens at judging / do we present on stage
A: At 2:15 judges visit each table. Finalists then give a three-minute demo on stage at 4.

# Q: Where can I get help with the glasses?
- also: who can help with the glasses / who do I ask for help
A: Dean from MicroLumin helps in the Discord's ask-for-help channel, or find a volunteer in a green lanyard.

# Q: What's the Wi-Fi?

A: The Wi-Fi network here is called "event".

# Q: Who is Dr. Dai?
- also: who is Dai Zhaoen / who founded MicroLumin
A: Dr. Dai Zhaoen founded MicroLumin, the company that makes these glasses. He's also a judge.

# Q: Who is Sam Shear?
A: Sam Shear co-founded Yes Hearing, which brings hearing care to people at home. He's a judge today.

# Q: Who is D.J. Patel?
- also: who is DJ Patel / who is DJP
A: D.J. Patel is a venture investor and three-time founder. He's a judge today.

# Q: Who is Yiting Liu?

- also: do we keep the glasses / when is hardware return
