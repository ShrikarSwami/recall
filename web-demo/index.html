Know who's in front of you.

Go-go is a memory assistant for people living with dementia. It lives on a pair of smart glasses, answers in a calm voice, and runs on the family's own computer.

No cloud. No account. No phone to dig out.

  • Answers come from the family's notes. For anything else, a model on our own hardware gives a short answer. It never guesses about people or health.
  • Faces are added with consent. Go-go only knows the people the family sets up.
  • The log stays on the family's computer. The only thing that leaves the house is a text to the caregiver when something's wrong.

What Go-go does

  • Tells you who someone is and answers follow-ups like “what's his favorite food?”
  • Gives the same calm answer every time. It never says “you already asked.”
  • Handles errands, like reading your list when you get to the store.
  • Shows your day, so you always know what's next.
  • Watches for falls (experimental) and texts your caregiver right away.
  • Starts the day with you. A short welcome so you know where you are.
